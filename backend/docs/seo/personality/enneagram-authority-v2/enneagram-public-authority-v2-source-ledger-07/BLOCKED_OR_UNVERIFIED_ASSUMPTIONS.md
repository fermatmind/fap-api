# Blocked or unverified assumptions

| Item | Status | Why blocked |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FermatMind reliability, validity, norms, percentiles, or instrument equivalence | Unknown | Published studies use other instruments, samples, languages, and scoring contracts |
| Universal nine-factor recovery or one fixed type per person | Unknown | The systematic review reports mixed evidence |
| Wing validity | Evidence-limited | The systematic review reports little supporting research |
| Instinctual-subtype universality or cross-cultural equivalence | Evidence-limited | One instrument-specific study cannot establish universal subtype ontology |
| Centers as biological or diagnostic systems | Blocked | Centers are traditional organizing vocabulary here |
| Career, hiring, relationship, health, admission, income, legal, or financial prediction | Forbidden | Outside the product boundary and unsupported |
| Truity claims as science or FermatMind authority | Not authority | Competitor material is intent-only |
| Named human reviewer or completed manual review | Unknown | Agent/model preparation is not human review |
| Current CMS publication, schema, sitemap, llms, or indexability eligibility | Blocked | Source preparation is not a release gate |
| Search ranking, traffic lift, or AI citation outcome | Unknown | Evidence structure cannot guarantee discovery or citation |

Unknown must not be coerced to false, zero, an actor identity, a percentage, or an unsupported assurance.
