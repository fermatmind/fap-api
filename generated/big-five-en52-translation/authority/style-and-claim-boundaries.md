# Big Five EN52 style and claim boundaries

Status: **locked for translation**

## Editorial voice

- Use natural, professional, plain American English for adult general readers.
- Lead with a direct answer, then explain evidence, limits, context, trade-offs, and practical reflection.
- Translate information units and meaning, not Chinese syntax. Avoid repetitive templates such as “This means that” and “People high in.”
- Preserve the FermatMind sequence of measurement, interpretation, action, and review.
- Keep each page independently useful without keyword stuffing or promises of ranking, indexing, or AI citation.

## Scientific boundaries

- Describe traits as continuous, probabilistic tendencies whose expression can vary by context.
- High, mid-range, and low are not value, ability, morality, diagnosis, or fixed-type categories.
- Correlation is not causation. Group-level findings do not predict an individual outcome.
- Model research does not establish FermatMind product reliability, validity, norms, percentiles, or predictive accuracy.
- Big Five content is for self-understanding and education. It is not clinical diagnosis, hiring or admissions screening, legal or financial advice, or an occupational outcome predictor.
- Neuroticism, Anxiety, and Depression are non-diagnostic trait labels in this framework. Do not infer a disorder.
- Openness is not intelligence; Agreeableness is not morality; Conscientiousness is not ability or guaranteed success; low Extraversion is not poor social skill.
- The 30 facets are the package’s explicitly identified NEO/IPIP-NEO interpretation framework, not a universal facet structure shared by all Big Five instruments.

## Translation equivalence

Every claim must use exactly one status:

- `exact_meaning_preserved`
- `localized_without_claim_change`
- `scientifically_narrowed`
- `needs_review`

Final assets may not contain `needs_review`. A scientifically narrowed claim must add no conclusion, must record why narrower wording was necessary, and must be included in the final scientific red-team report.

## Sources and originality

- Preserve the Chinese page’s source IDs and claim relationships.
- Use formal English bibliographic titles; do not translate official publication titles back from Chinese.
- Do not invent a DOI, URL, author, journal, reliability coefficient, validity statistic, norm, percentile, or causal conclusion.
- Competitor pages may not be used as scientific sources, wording sources, paragraph templates, metaphors, headings, or FAQ templates.

## SEO/GEO content boundary

- Optimize only visible editorial fields already present in the content-package contract.
- Titles, descriptions, introductions, headings, FAQs, sources, and internal-link anchors must be natural, unique, and visible.
- FAQ answers may not introduce unsupported new claims.
- Visible evidence and answerability do not prove graph authority, ranking, citation, or AI planning.
- Do not add or change runtime metadata, canonical, hreflang, robots, sitemap, llms, llms-full, JSON-LD, or hidden schema.

## Permanent technical boundary

No page may contain hero, inline, OG, Twitter, Markdown, HTML, or other media fields. No CMS write, production write, working revision, promotion, deployment, cache operation, Search submission, API wire-shape change, fap-web change, or legacy alias page is authorized by this package.
