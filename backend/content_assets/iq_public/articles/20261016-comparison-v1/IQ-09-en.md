## If you need membership or formal documentation

Start with the organisation that will accept the result. Mensa practice is distinct from admission: British Mensa's [free Workout](https://mensa.org.uk/test-your-iq/) explicitly offers no IQ score, while its [supervised online service](https://mensa.org.uk/mensa-online-iq-test/) has its own residence, equipment and proctoring conditions. “Online” alone does not tell you whether a service is accepted. Our [Mensa practice and admission guide](/en/articles/mensa-iq-test-free-practice-vs-admission) explains those distinctions.

For a school, clinical or other formal assessment requirement, establish the required instrument, language, age suitability, practitioner and report with the receiving organisation or qualified professional. A practice certificate or a free score cannot establish acceptance.

Do not subtract or average scores across these sites. Different questions, references, languages and conditions make the numbers non-interchangeable without relevant comparability evidence. This review tests what a user can access; it does not identify the “most accurate” tool.
