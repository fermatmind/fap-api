# Big Five Authority V2 — Article Stress and Wellbeing 30

This package consumes only PR21 batch 30 and produces ten backend-authoritative `Article` draft candidates across stress response, self-regulation, recovery, daily support, and help-boundary themes in EN and `zh-CN`.

The assets are non-medical and non-diagnostic. They never equate neuroticism with mental illness, assess symptoms or crisis risk, prescribe treatment, guarantee wellbeing, or replace qualified professional, local emergency, or crisis help. All FermatMind clinical and psychometric claims remain `Unknown`; private reports and attempt links remain excluded.

Raw drafts, skeptical reviews, repairs, final assets, source mappings, and QA are preserved. Every candidate remains `pending_manual_review` with null attribution and zero CMS, publication, or indexability mutation.

Repository rule impact: none. These are CMS/backend-authoritative draft candidates and add no frontend fallback or publishing authority.
