# Career editorial review rubric

## Source and claim boundary

- Identify the source, version/date, market, methodology, and field or passage supported.
- Preserve the difference between an official classification/statistic, a sourced interpretation, and FermatMind editorial guidance.
- Omit or qualify unsupported claims. Never manufacture experience evidence, author credentials, citations, salary samples, demand, rankings, or outcomes.
- Treat AI impact as task- and condition-specific analysis, not a guaranteed replacement date or employment outcome.
- Treat RIASEC, Big Five, MBTI, IQ, and other personality/cognitive material as exploratory and non-deterministic. Do not rank a person's employability or suitability from a type or score.
- Keep market and period labels on salary, growth, licensing, and hiring claims. A foreign-market figure must not be relabeled as Chinese evidence.

## Reader quality

- The page should answer occupation-specific questions with concrete duties, environments, tools, tradeoffs, and next steps.
- Avoid template-only substitution, repetitive section openings, vague attribution, inflated authority, invented urgency, and promotional superlatives.
- Preserve visible FAQ question/answer pairing, table headers and units, source links, canonical internal-link identity, and truthful CTA destination.
- Do not add JobPosting, rating, review, schema, canonical, hreflang, sitemap, llms, or search behavior as an editorial shortcut.
- Unknown or missing evidence is stated as unknown or omitted; it is not filled from another occupation or a local template.

## Decision

- `PASS`: no material factual, claim-boundary, completeness, or reader-quality defect.
- `WARN`: non-blocking improvement is recommended and the current authorized artifact remains usable.
- `BLOCKED`: a material unsupported claim, source mismatch, privacy issue, discriminatory/deterministic claim, incomplete translation/component, or misleading reader action must be corrected before this new or edited artifact proceeds.

These decisions apply only to the reviewed candidate. They do not revoke or hide previously approved canonical content and do not authorize publication.
