# Quick-decision source routing

Use the Career Research Producer's `references/source-policy.md` as the source-tier authority. This file identifies which parts of each source are useful for the quick-decision reasoning chain.

## Preferred evidence

| Need | Preferred source | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Occupation identity and task boundary | O*NET Online/Data, US SOC, 中国《职业分类大典》, ILO ISCO, EU ESCO, equivalent official classifications | Confirm title, code, task scope, combined/child occupations, and adjacent-role boundaries |
| Repeated duties and work products | O*NET Tasks/Work Activities, BLS OOH “What They Do”, official occupational standards | Identify what the worker repeatedly produces, checks, decides, repairs, teaches, or communicates |
| Work setting and demands | O*NET Work Context, BLS OOH “Work Environment” and Occupational Requirements Survey, official safety bodies | Support pace, schedule, physical exposure, interaction, consequence-of-error, and environmental constraints |
| Interests and work patterns | O*NET Interests, Work Styles, Work Values; transparent public occupational research | Supply probabilistic signals only; translate them through real tasks and never treat them as selection scores |
| China-specific scope | Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security, National Bureau of Statistics, relevant ministries, regulators, national occupational standards | Confirm official Chinese occupation naming, tasks, qualification or regulatory boundaries, and dated local context |
| Regulated practice | Government regulator, statute/rule publisher, standards body, licensing authority | Confirm what requires a credential, who may perform the work, and jurisdiction/effective date |
| Current tools or hiring signals | Dated public employer pages or recruitment samples | Use only as bounded market signals for tools/workflow; never as official prevalence, pay, national demand, or proof of personal fit |
| Work change or AI effects | Peer-reviewed or high-quality institutional task research plus versioned FermatMind rubric | Explain task change separately from the fit decision; never equate exposure with job loss |

Useful official entry points include `onetonline.org` / `onetcenter.org`, `bls.gov/ooh` and `bls.gov/ors`, `mohrss.gov.cn`, `stats.gov.cn`, occupation-specific Chinese regulator domains, `ilo.org` for ISCO, and the European Commission's ESCO service. Resolve the exact current page during research and record its URL, publisher, version/data year, jurisdiction, scope, retrieval date, and content hash.

## Minimum evidence for this visual group

- At least one Tier 1 task or work-activity source matching the occupation scope.
- At least one official work-context, work-environment, occupational-requirements, safety, or professional-duty source. It may be the same publisher but must support a different premise.
- Tier 1 or 2 evidence for every regulated, licensing, safety, legal, medical, or financial-practice premise.
- Source keys for every factual premise used to justify `summary`, `suit`, or `boundary`.
- An explicit Tier 5 `editorial_synthesis` or `conditional_guidance` binding for the final fit interpretation and the `how` experiment.

If an exact source is unavailable, preserve the honest scope (`combined_official`, parent proxy, or unresolved). Missing work-context evidence blocks a confident fit judgment; it is not replaced by job-board copy, a competitor page, an AI answer, or another career's wording.

## Competitor and search research

CareerExplorer, CareerOneStop, Prospects, National Careers Service, Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, university career pages, and comparable career-detail products may be reviewed to discover reader questions, terminology, page structure, and content gaps. Unless a source independently qualifies under the Career source policy, it is not evidence for a factual or scientific claim.

Real GSC queries, search suggestions, and public search-result patterns may identify whether readers ask “适合什么人”, “压力大吗”, or “怎么判断”. They may change ordering and phrasing only. Do not infer search volume, occupational truth, or a personality relationship from them.
